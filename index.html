Camera

Ctrl (or ⌘) + drag orbits the camera from anywhere — no need to switch tools. Shift + drag slides the view, and the wheel zooms toward the cursor.

Build precisely

Distance is the semi-major axis from the parent. The start angle advances automatically after each add, so successive bodies don't stack on top of each other.

Place by dragging

In Add mode, drag on empty space: where you press is the position, the drag direction and length set the launch velocity. The green line previews the resulting path.
